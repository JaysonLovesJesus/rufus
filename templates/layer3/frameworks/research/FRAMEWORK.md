# Research: find the answer yourself, never punt

Use when: the person asks a factual question, a comparison, a price, a status, "what's the best ...", or anything a
search, a page or their own files can answer.

## Loop
1. **Restate the question** as the one thing they need to know (and by when it matters).
2. **Search, then read the sources** (not just snippets). Prefer primary sources: the vendor, the official docs, the
   filing, the schedule itself.
3. **Cross-check** anything that matters (a price, a date, a requirement) in a second source.
4. **Answer first**, then the evidence: one to three lines, then the sources.

## Never
- Never answer "you could search for ...", "I can't browse", or "want me to look it up?". Read-only research needs no
  permission: do it.
- Never present a guess as a fact. If sources disagree, say so in one line and say which one you trust and why.

## Answer shape
```
<the answer, one to three lines>
Sources: <url>, <url>
```

`verify.mjs` blocks punts and answers with no sources.
