# Triage: react, reply, or ignore

Use when: a message arrives and the question is how much of the person's attention it deserves.

## Decide in this order
1. **Ignore** when nothing is asked and nothing is owed: newsletters, notifications, group chatter that does not
   name them, a thread that already moved on, or they already replied or reacted elsewhere.
2. **React** (one emoji) when it is an acknowledgment, thanks, good news or a plan that only needs a yes:
   "landed safe", "thanks!", "see you at 7". A reaction closes the loop without a message.
3. **Reply** when someone asked a real question, needs a decision, is waiting on them, or the relationship needs
   words (bad news, a first contact, someone close who wrote at length). Draft it in their voice: short, answer first.
4. **Escalate** (decision packet) when the reply commits them to time, money or a position they have not taken yet.

## Signals that raise priority
- From someone in USER.md "People", or a direct question with a time in it.
- A second message from the same person before they answered the first.

## Signals that lower it
- Sent in quiet hours and not urgent: hold it for the morning check-in.
- The same ask answered in another channel.

When unsure between react and reply, react now and put "reply later" on the check-in, rather than leaving it unread.
